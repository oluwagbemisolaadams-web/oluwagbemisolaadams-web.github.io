About my work

I test a payment application used for wallets, transfers, crypto, virtual cards and identity checks. In this work a wrong balance, a missing fee or a lost transaction record is a serious defect, so I check every amount against what the user sees, what the API returns and what the history says.

I test on Web, iOS and Android, and I test APIs in Postman before the frontend is connected. Before QA I worked as a virtual assistant, which taught me to work remotely, document clearly and follow through without being chased.

Issues I reported

Receiving through Add XAF gives no notification and no history

High | Flow: Receive, Add XAF

Push notifications work elsewhere in the app, so this is specific to this flow. Expected: a push and a transaction entry as soon as the money arrives.

A Francophone account cannot send to a bank or mobile money

High | Flow: Send, Bank account / Mobile Money

The country list has Nigeria only, and the option says Mobile Money but offers Bank Accounts only. Expected: Francophone countries and mobile money providers.

Convert history is wrong

Medium | Flow: Transactions, Transaction Details

Only a credit appears, with no debit for the USDT sent, and the details read "+BTC 0.00" with the generic description "Transaction". Expected: both sides, the real amount and a clear description such as "Converted USDT to BTC".

Fee shown in the summary is not in the total

Medium | Flow: Convert, Payment Summary

I raised this as a product decision: confirm whether the fee is charged, then show it in the total.

Wrong balance on the Add XAF screen

Low | Flow: Receive Money

Shows an available balance before the wallet exists. Expected: the correct balance, or none.

How I test APIs

My Postman collections belong to my employer, so I do not share them. This is how I work with them.

  • I organise collections in folders by feature area, such as authentication, wallet, cards and transactions.
  • I document every endpoint the same way: name, description, method and URL, headers, body, parameters and response notes.
  • I check status codes, response fields, error messages and validation, not only the happy path.
  • I sign off the API before the frontend team starts integration testing, and I confirm money movements against the database.
